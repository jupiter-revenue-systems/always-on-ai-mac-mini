## Step 9. Turn on Screen Sharing and Remote Login

On the Mac mini:

1. Open **System Settings → General → Sharing**.
2. Turn on **Screen Sharing**.
3. Turn on **Remote Login**.
4. Leave **File Sharing** and **Remote Management** off. You don't need them, and less remote access is safer.
5. Check that the name at the top is your Mac mini's name. If it isn't, change it here.
