## Pick these before you start

| What | Example |
|---|---|
| Your Mac mini's name | Atlas |
| Its main user's full name | Atlas Admin |
| Its account name (lowercase, no spaces) | atlasadmin |
| Your business folder in iCloud Drive | My Business |

In the steps below, `<name>` means your Mac mini's name and `<account>` means its account name.

## Step 1. Unbox it

If you're filming the unboxing, blur the serial number and barcode before you post it.

## Step 2. Plug it in

1. Plug the power cord into your battery backup, not straight into the wall.
2. Run Ethernet from your router into the Mac mini.
3. Plug in the monitor (HDMI or USB-C), keyboard and mouse.
4. Press the power button. It's on the bottom, at the back left corner.
